Aloha, and welcome

North Shore Hackerspace is a small, genuine hackerspace on the North Shore of Oahu — a dedicated room in Waialua, just down the road from Haleiwa Town. We opened in March 2023 on a simple idea: the people building things out here deserve a place to build them together.

One room, a real bench, and the door open. Bring your project, your soldering habit, your half-finished firmware, your radio gear. Find some quiet, find some space, find someone to think out loud with.

The hours run 9 to 6, which leaves the morning for the hard part and the evening for the water. Plenty of good work has been done on that schedule.

Everyone is welcome — visitors passing through and ohana who call the North Shore home. Day, weekly, and monthly passes are all available, and all priced to be reachable.

Finally, a genuine Haleiwa-based hackerspace.

Create

Bring the half-finished thing. Firmware that will not flash, a board that needs reflowing, a model that will not converge. Bench space, quiet, and room to think it through.

Connect

Makers, developers, radio operators, and tinkerers who actually live and work out here. Small enough that you will know everyone — which is the point.

Balance

Island hours, kept on purpose. Get deep into something hard in the morning and still make the water before the light goes.
